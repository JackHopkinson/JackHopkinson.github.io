Morrow privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Morrow (com.bytesandbobs.morrow) is published by BytesAndBobs. Contact: bytesandbobs.dev@gmail.com.

What Morrow stores

The names, amounts, currencies, and dates you enter, plus your preferences and cached exchange rates, are stored in a database on your device. BytesAndBobs does not receive this data and has no accounts or servers for it. Morrow does not connect to your bank or financial accounts.

Android backup

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include Morrow's database in your Google account backup and restore it on a new device. This is controlled by Google and your device settings, and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Exchange rates

To convert currencies, Morrow requests rates from the Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev), sending only the valuation dates and currency codes needed. Your names and balances are never sent. Like any internet request, the service can see your IP address.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. BytesAndBobs does not receive your payment details. Morrow asks Google Play which purchases your account owns in order to unlock Premium.

What Morrow does not do

No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, tracking, or data sales. Morrow does not share your data with third parties.

Your choices and data deletion

You can edit or remove any entry, or use Settings → Delete all data in the app to erase everything stored by Morrow on your device. Uninstalling the app also removes local data. To remove an existing Google backup, manage backups in your Google account settings. Because BytesAndBobs holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Morrow is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and in the app.
